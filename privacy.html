Your privacy

Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who helps us run the site, how long we keep it, and how you can change or delete it. Effective 19 October 2026.

Who we are

The operator

The Shelf of Daniel is a free, guided study of the book of Daniel. It is run by Fanelesibonge Ndlovu, who is the operator and the controller of the personal information described on this page.

Contact us at shelfofdaniel@gmail.com. Signed-in readers can also write to the operator: sign in, open Messages, and choose Write to us.

Please read this policy together with our Terms of use.

The short version

What we do and do not do

  • We take no payments. Nothing on the site costs money, and there is no checkout.
  • We show no advertising and use no advertising trackers.
  • We do not record your screen and we do not replay your sessions.
  • We send no marketing email.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • Your email address is used only to sign you in, to confirm your account, and to reset your password.

Without an account

What we collect when you read anonymously

You can read the Prologue, Daniel 1, and Daniel 2 without signing in. While you read, the site may record:

  • study events, such as which lessons you open, which steps of a sitting you visit, how far down a page you scroll, and how long you spend on a lesson;
  • the sitting number, the page name, and the time of each event;
  • a random anonymous ID kept in your browser, and a session ID that ends when you close the tab;
  • your optional answer to the short sitting question (a score from 1 to 5 and an optional note);
  • technical reports when a request is blocked or the browser reports a security rule being broken. These name the rule, not your content.

Our hosting provider sees your IP address on every request, as every web host does. We use it to limit abuse. We do not write your IP address into our database.

With an account

What we collect when you sign in

  • Account details: your email address and a password stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a Google account ID.
  • Authenticator app: if an authenticator app was added to your account, its setup and factor record are kept by Supabase.
  • Study progress: the sittings you have finished, your answers to quiz questions, your workbench entries, your mastery marks, and competency records, so you can continue on another device.
  • Notes and bookmarks: the notes you write on each sheet, with the time you last changed them, and the sheets you bookmark.
  • Certificate: the name you choose for your certificate, its serial number, and the date it was issued.
  • Optional profile answers: how you are studying (alone, in a group, or teaching a class), your background (Protestant, Catholic, other, or prefer not to say), and how you found the site.
  • Referrals: your referral code, and the referral you arrived by if you followed someone else's link.
  • Reviews: the display name, text, and star rating you post. Approved reviews are shown publicly.
  • Messages: what you send to the operator, and the operator's replies.
  • Surveys and events: your survey answers and study events, linked to your account.
  • Sign-in safety: a count of failed password attempts over a short window, to slow down guessing.
  • Terms: the version of the terms you accepted and when you accepted them.

Cookies and local storage

What the site stores in your browser

The site does not set tracking cookies. It keeps small pieces of information in your browser's local storage, and some in session storage, which clears when you close the tab. Most of it keeps your place, your settings, and your study on this device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings, but you may lose your place and any notes stored only on this device.

Cloudflare may set its own strictly necessary security cookies on the sites it protects.

On your first visit a banner asks whether you allow analytics. Analytics means the study events described above, the anonymous ID that groups them, and Cloudflare Web Analytics. These start only if you choose Accept. Reject is as easy as Accept, and if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track we treat that as Reject. Everything else in the table below is needed for the site to work and does not need your consent.

Your choice is saved in local storage under the key sodConsent, so we do not ask again on every page. You can change it at any time with Cookie settings at the foot of the page. If you withdraw consent, the anonymous ID and any unsent events are removed from your browser.

When you sign out, the site first saves anything not yet sent to your account, then removes your notes, bookmarks, progress, reading position and certificate name from that browser, so the next person on a shared computer cannot read them. They come back when you sign in again.

Who can see it

Access inside the site

The operator can see the information stored for the site. Moderators approved by the operator can see a list of graduates. That list includes each graduate's confirmed email address, certificate name, sittings completed, last activity, and certificate download time. The same moderators can see the confirmed email address and message of anyone who writes to the operator.

Approved reviews are public. Your display name, text, rating, and date appear on the Reviews page.

Our service providers see only what is described in the table above.

Transfers

International transfers

Our providers run servers in several countries, including the United States. Your information may therefore be processed outside your own country, including outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and South Africa. Where the law requires it, we rely on the transfer safeguards these providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

What you can ask us to do

  • Access: get a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: fix something that is wrong. You can change your certificate name on the Account page.
  • Deletion: delete your account and the personal information tied to it.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests, including study events.
  • Restriction: limit how we use your information while a request is being considered.
  • Portability: receive your information in a format you can take elsewhere.
  • Withdrawing consent: withdraw any consent at any time. This does not undo anything we did while the consent was in place.

Email shelfofdaniel@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We will reply within one month, as the GDPR and UK GDPR require. A normal request costs nothing.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. If you are in the European Economic Area, you may complain to your national data protection authority. If you are in South Africa, you may complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za under POPIA. You may complain to a regulator without contacting us first, but we would welcome the chance to help first.

Deleting your account

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in and open the Account page.
  2. If you want a copy first, choose Download my data.
  3. Under Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account and the information tied to it are erased at once. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in, email shelfofdaniel@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within one month.

Deletion removes your sign-in, progress, notes, bookmarks, certificate record, profile, quiz answers, reviews, messages, survey answers, and failed sign-in records, and removes the link between you and your study events. The items that can remain for a time are listed in the retention table above. Anything stored only on your own device stays there until you clear your browser's storage.

Children

Age requirements

The Shelf of Daniel is for people aged 13 and over. In parts of the European Economic Area, the age is 16. If you are below the age that applies where you live, you may use the site only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

We do not knowingly collect information from younger children. If you believe a child has given us information without permission, email us and we will delete it.

Security

How we protect your information

The site is served over HTTPS only, with a content security policy that limits which services each page may contact. Database access is limited by row-level security rules, so most records can be read only by their owner. Passwords are stored as scrambled values. We limit how often data can be written, check for abuse at the edge of the network, and use a human check at sign-in.

No system is perfectly secure. Use a strong password and keep it private. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Changes

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes, and we will change the effective date at the top. If a change affects how we use your information in a significant way, we will tell signed-in readers on the site before it takes effect.
