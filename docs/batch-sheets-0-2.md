## s0-2 — The Biblical Year-Day Scale

### step 1 — Forty days become forty years
- share: one prophetic measure, fixed before Daniel uses it
- ahead: Numbers fixes the measure in the wilderness: forty days of spying, forty years of wandering. The next movement brings the second witness, Ezekiel 4:6, where the same measure is given again centuries later. Neither witness is Daniel, and the rule is on record before he uses it.

### step 2 — One scale, stated twice
- share: the same ruler, now carried into Daniel's seventy weeks
- ahead: Two witnesses have fixed the measure outside Daniel. The next movement brings it to Daniel's seventy weeks, where read as days they end nowhere near Messiah the Prince, and read as years they run to His cross. Daniel 9 is the test case; there the reckoning is tested, before it is carried into spans that history has to confirm.

## s2-1 — The Prophetic Master-Framework (Daniel 2)

### step 1 — Babylon
- share: one chain: a named head, then the kingdom after it
- ahead: Verse 38 names the head of the image, so the chest and arms that follow are the next kingdom in order rather than a symbol that can be restarted in any century. The record matches the metals: Babylon falls to the Medes and Persians, and the two arms answer the two-handed empire that replaced it.

### step 2 — Medo-Persia
- share: two kingdoms Daniel 8 names outright, the ram and the goat
- ahead: The silver is not left to inference: Daniel 8:20 names the ram as the kings of Media and Persia. The next movement takes the third metal, bronze, and the same chapter names it too — the rough goat, the king of Grecia. That is how the sequence is read without guessing.

### step 3 — Greece
- share: Bronze carries a breadth of rule, and iron breaks what it touches
- ahead: Bronze carries a breadth of rule the first two kingdoms did not have. The next movement takes the iron that follows it — a kingdom described by what it does to everything it touches, its two legs standing for an empire that divides in the end into east and west. One year closes the bronze and opens the iron: in 168, at Pydna, Rome ended the monarchy.

### step 4 — Rome
- share: one kingdom, still iron, now in a divided dress
- ahead: The legs are two, and the empire they stand for divides in the end into east and west. The next movement shows that division inside the image: no fifth metal appears, only iron mixed with miry clay, and Daniel 2:43 says the mixture will not hold. This is the last political state of the iron.

### step 5 — Divided Europe
- share: a strike at the feet that breaks the whole image
- ahead: The feet are the only part of the image God's kingdom strikes, and Daniel 2:43 says the mixture will not hold. The next movement is the Stone: cut out without hands, no human power quarrying it, striking the feet in the days of these kings. It breaks the whole image at once and becomes a mountain that fills the earth — the kingdom does not arrive by the sword.
