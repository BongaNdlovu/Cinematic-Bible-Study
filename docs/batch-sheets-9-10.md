## s9-4 — The 69 Weeks to Messiah the Prince (457 B.C. to A.D. 27)
### step 1 — Decree of Artaxerxes I
- share: the seven weeks: 49 years from the decree to the rebuilt city
- ahead: Of the four Persian commissions this chart compares, only this one restores city and state — what Daniel 9:25 asks for when it says "to restore and to build Jerusalem". Its 49 years are the rebuilding that follows, and 9:25 puts that work "in troublous times".
### step 2 — Jerusalem Rebuilt
- share: the sixty-two weeks: 434 years from the rebuilt city to the baptism
- ahead: The two spans are consecutive, not overlapping, so the 434 years run on from the rebuilt city to the Jordan. Sixty-nine of the seventy weeks land there, "a dated decree at one end, a dated baptism at the other" — the checkpoint the reckoning rests on. One week of the seventy remains, and it opens at this same baptism.

## s9-5 — The 70th Week of Daniel (A.D. 27 to A.D. 34)
### step 1 — Baptism of Jesus
- share: the one seventieth week, and the cross at its midst
- ahead: The seventieth week opens where the sixty-ninth closed — the baptism that completes the sixty-nine weeks is the same event that opens the seventieth. Its first half is the ministry to Israel, 3.5 years running to the Passover. The sitting reads the cross it reaches as "the immovable anchor that validates the terminal date of the 2,300 days".
### step 2 — The Cross (Passover)
- share: the remaining half-week: the cross opens it, the stoning closes it
- ahead: Here the week reaches its midst, where Messiah is cut off and the sacrifice and oblation cease. The cross does not end the seventieth week: its remaining 3.5 years run on, and the gospel goes to the Gentiles as the weeks expire. The close comes with the stoning of Stephen, which the sitting reads as closing the seventy weeks allotted to Daniel's people and city — inferred from the half-week and from Acts, not from a year the narrative prints.
