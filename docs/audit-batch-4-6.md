## MUST FIX

1. `s5-2 step 2 ahead` — "already serving in the palace". No cited source supports it: Daniel 5:2 is about the drinking, Daniel 5:13 has Daniel brought in, and the sitting's own line is "When Daniel was summoned".
2. `s4-1 step 1 share` — "only the last is a man's own decree". It denies 3:29 the status the card's own step 2 detail gives it ("his own verdict comes at 3:29") and that the KJV gives it ("Therefore I make a decree"). Use the pack's predicate: "and only the last costs him his glory".
3. `s4-1 step 2` — the correction lives in the panel only. `share` and `ahead` both assign the words of faith to the Hebrews, but the card keeps the title quoting 3:17–18 as the king's, and the same cite defect runs through step 1 (title quotes 2:47, cited 2:38) and step 3 ("the King of heaven" is 4:37, cited 4:34–35). Cite 2:47 / 3:29 / 4:37.
4. `s4-1 step 2 share` — "counterpart". The pack makes 3:17–18 the answer given *before* the king's confession; "counterpart" re-pairs the two sayings, which is the pairing the `ahead` undoes.
5. `s5-2 step 2 ahead` — "History:" prefix over a verse fact (the offer, 5:7/5:16). Split the label so history reads as history and the offer reads as verse.
6. `s4-3 step 2 ahead` — "can only go further". Step 3 `text` asserts what the clause does, not that nothing else could follow.
7. Rule 4 (four of six `share` lines are not sentences, so they cannot stand alone in the backward panel): s4-1 step 2, s4-3 step 2, s5-2 step 1, s5-2 step 2. Give each a subject and a verb, or accept that the panel is unreadable without the card.

## The three flagged items

**s4-1 step 2 — misattribution.** Half-fixed. The `ahead` states the correction outright ("The words of faith here belong to the three Hebrews, not to the king") and reassigns the king's own words to 3:29; the `share` also gives the words of faith to the Hebrews.
But the fix exists only in the panel and never reaches the card, so a learner who reads only the card still meets the title quoting Daniel 3:17–18 as the king's — and steps 1 and 3 carry the same defect.

**s4-3 step 2→3 — cross-reference.** Licensed, and the author's worry is overstated. Sentence 1 is step 2 `detail` verbatim; sentence 2 restates step 3's `text`, which is the sitting's own claim, so nothing is asserted that the sitting does not assert.
The only word at fault is "only" ("can only go further"), a necessity the `text` does not carry; and a stated clause was available anyway — Daniel 4:30's "for the honour of my majesty" (step 3's title) read with step 1 `detail`'s mapping to 2:37's gifts.

**s5-2 — thinnest ground.** Both transitions stay inside the card except for the two faults above: the card's claim (Nabonidus first, Belshazzar second, the highest office available third) is restated faithfully in both shares, and Daniel 5:7 is cited as verse, not history.
The discipline slip is narrow — the verse offer sits under a "History:" prefix in the step 2 `ahead` and under a "(History)" tag in the step 2 `share`; 5:7 and 5:16 are nowhere called history, and no bridge line adds a date or a source the card does not carry.
