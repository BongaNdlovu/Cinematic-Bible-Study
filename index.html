DETAILED EXPLANATION

The golden head represents the Babylonian Empire, the first in a series of world empires foretold by God. Babylon was known for its wealth, wisdom, and splendor, which is why gold is used to depict its rule.

HISTORICAL BACKGROUND

Under Nebuchadnezzar II, Babylon became the greatest city of its time. He expanded his empire, rebuilt the city with magnificent structures, and made Babylon the center of world power.
