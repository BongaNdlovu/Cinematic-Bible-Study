Why it matters today

  1. God is in control of human history.
  2. It helps us read the events of our own time.
  3. It strengthens faith when the world is uncertain.
  4. It warns against pride, oppression, and moral decline.
  5. It points to the return of Christ and God’s eternal kingdom.
