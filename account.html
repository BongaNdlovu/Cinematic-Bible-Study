Welcome

The Shelf of Daniel is a guided study of the book of Daniel, offered for personal reading and classroom teaching. It is not a church membership, a diploma, or a paid course, and no payment is taken here. You may browse the study, map, and gallery without an account. You may read the first sittings — the Prologue, Daniel 1, and Daniel 2 — without an account. The later sittings, the Map of History, and the 3D Symbols gallery ask you to sign in.

When sign-in helps

Sign in to unlock the sittings after Daniel 2, to open the Map of History and the 3D Symbols gallery, to save progress in the cloud, to write a review, and to print a certificate. Signing in lets us recognize you on a later visit so you do not have to sign in every time. You may sign out at any time and keep reading the free sittings.

Certificate of completion

Signing in is required so the certificate of completion can carry your proper details. Your signed-in name is used on the certificate; you may confirm or correct it before you download. Without a signed-in profile the exhibit cannot print a certificate that belongs to you.

How your study is kept

Your place in the sitting and your progress stay on this device while you browse. After you sign in they are also saved with your profile so you can continue where you left off on another device.

What we learn from your study

While you study, the exhibit records simple study events: which lessons you open and finish, which steps of a sitting you visit, your answers to checkpoint questions, and the time spent on a lesson. Use is anonymous until sign-in. With your signed-in profile, your study progress and optional answers are collected to improve the lessons. No selling, no advertising. To ask for deletion, email the site operator at admin@endoftime7.com.

Scripture and the exhibit

Bible quotations are from the King James Version. The lessons, maps, images, and reconstructions are for study on this site. You may not copy the exhibit to sell, scrape, or republish as your own.

What this site is not

  • We do not offer legal, medical, or pastoral counsel.
  • Dates and historical notes are taught with care, but scholars may still differ.
  • The exhibit is a teaching aid. It does not replace the reading of Scripture itself.

Your agreement

By pressing Accept you state that you have read these terms, that you are old enough to use the site, and that you agree before you sign in. If the terms change, you will be asked to agree again.
