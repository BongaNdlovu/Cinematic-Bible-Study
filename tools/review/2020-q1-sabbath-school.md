# Adult Bible Study Guide — Daniel, 1st Quarter 2020: analysis & what the course can use

Analyzed 2026-09-14 from the publicly published lesson pages
([archive index](https://ssnet.org/study-guides/lesson-archives/2020-2029/2020-q1-daniel/),
lessons 1–13 at ssnet.org/lessons/20a/). **Author / principal contributor: Elias Brasil de Souza,
director of the Biblical Research Institute.** This is the single closest BRI-line document to the
eleven sittings that exists — it is the church's own chapter-by-chapter treatment of the same book
on the same historicist line, prepared under BRI leadership.

**Copyright status:** © General Conference of Seventh-day Adventists (Sabbath School/Adult Bible
Study Guide). This document stores **analysis, structure, and short attributed citations only** —
no wholesale lesson text. Rules for the project are at the bottom.

## The quarterly at a glance

Thirteen weeks, one per chapter, each titled "From X to Y." Memory texts are NKJV. Every lesson
pairs exposition with Ellen White and ends with Further Thought. The doctrinal spine matches the
course's: four prophetic cycles (Dan 2, 7, 8–9, 10–12) repeating Babylon → Medo-Persia → Greece →
Rome → God's kingdom; year-day; papal little horn with 538–1798; 2,300 years anchored at 457 B.C.
through the 70 weeks to 1844; pre-advent judgment; Michael = Christ; bodily resurrection.

## Verdict: what can and should be used

**Can be used freely (no permission needed):**
1. **As a named source.** Cite "Adult Bible Study Guide, *Daniel* (1st Q 2020), week N" in verify
   packs and review reports wherever the course's claim matches the church's published line. It is
   the strongest available citation for: ribs = Lydia/Babylon/Egypt; Rome pagan-then-papal; 538–1798;
   A.D. 34 Stephen; 508 start for 1,290/1,335; Michael = Christ with judicial "stand."
2. **Facts, verse anchors, and teaching frameworks** (four cycles, genre distinction, Atonement-ritual
   link of ram/goat, Shinar-Babel, Isa 14:4 gold, Diodorus Siculus). Ideas and facts are not
   copyrightable; say them in the course's own words.
3. **Short attributed quotes** (a sentence or two, named source) in the internal `tools/` documents —
   already the practice in this folder (e.g., SDABC 4:841 above).
4. **Linking.** A "Compare: Sabbath School lesson on Daniel 8 (2020 Q1)" line with the public URL is
   the cleanest way to hand students the official companion.

**Should be done in this project (recommended next change):** add the quarterly as a citation on the
sitting-7 bear-ribs row, the sitting-8 two-phase-Rome row, the sitting-9 A.D.-34 row, and the
sitting-10 508 row (all previously flagged "Needs citation" in the reports — this closes them), plus
the five enrichments the course lacks (Shinar–Babel; Isa 14:4 gold; Atonement-ritual ram/goat;
Diodorus Siculus; the 1 Cor 15 deliverance guard rail).

**Cannot / should not be done:**
- Reproducing lesson prose, day-by-day text, or PDFs in this repo or on the public site.
- Quoting the quarterly at length anywhere public. Brief attributed excerpts only.
- Using NKJV memory-text wording in the site's data beyond verse-length quotation; the course
  correctly uses KJV (public domain) for its Scripture quotes — keep that practice.
- Anything beyond brief quotation for public redistribution would need permission from the General
  Conference / Adult Bible Study Guide (the text is freely *published*, but it is not freely
  *re-licensable*).

## Discrepancy log (nuances, not conflicts)

1. Gold-head start: quarterly 626 B.C. vs course 605 B.C. (course follows Daniel 1:1's own datum).
2. Daniel 8 vision date: quarterly 548/547 B.C. vs course c. 551 B.C. (access-year conventions).
3. 1844 day: quarterly gives the year only (1844) and defers chronology to week 10; the course adds
   October 22 by Karaite reckoning, explicitly labeled as its finer claim.
4. Register: the quarterly has no Reformation-genealogy layer (Trent, Alcázar, Ribera, Darby). The
   course goes beyond BRI there — which the sitting-0 report already requires to be labeled as
   history, and it is.
