## 1. The Anchor Date: The Decree of Artaxerxes I (457 B.C.)

### The Biblical Commandment
- Daniel 9:25: "from the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build Jerusalem unto the Messiah the Prince shall be seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks."
- Three Persian decrees concerned the return to Judah:
  1. **Cyrus (536 B.C. / Ezra 1):** Permitted return to rebuild the *temple* only.
  2. **Darius I (519 B.C. / Ezra 6):** Confirmed Cyrus’s temple decree.
  3. **Artaxerxes I Longimanus (457 B.C. / Ezra 7:7–26):** Fully authorized Ezra to re-establish civil judicial administration ("set magistrates and judges... to judge all the people", Ezra 7:25), enforce capital and civil punishment, and restore Jerusalem as an autonomous self-governing city-state (Ezra 9:9).

### Chronological Verification of 457 B.C.
1. **The 7th Year of Artaxerxes I:**
   - Ezra 7:7–8: Ezra went up to Jerusalem in the "seventh year of Artaxerxes the king" (arriving in the 5th month).
   - Artaxerxes I succeeded Xerxes I after the latter's death in late December 465 B.C.
   - Under the Persian-Babylonian spring-to-spring calendar, Artaxerxes' accession year ran to Nisan 464 B.C., making his 1st regnal year 464/463 B.C., and his 7th regnal year 458/457 B.C.
   - Under the Jewish fall-to-fall civil calendar (Tishri-to-Tishri, utilized by Nehemiah in Neh 1:1 and Neh 2:1), Artaxerxes' accession year ran to Tishri 464 B.C., his 1st year was Tishri 464 to Tishri 463 B.C., and his 7th year was Tishri 458 to Tishri 457 B.C. Ezra's journey in the spring/summer of the 7th year places the decree and its enactment firmly in **457 B.C.**
2. **Primary Documentation:**
   - Siegfried H. Horn and Lynn H. Wood, *The Chronology of Ezra 7: A Report Which Solves a Chronological Problem* (Washington, D.C.: Review and Herald, 1953; 2nd ed. 1970).
   - Double-dated Aramaic papyri from Elephantine, Egypt (e.g. Kraeling Papyrus 6; Cowley Papyrus 30) demonstrating that Jews in the Persian Empire consistently reckoned regnal years by the civil autumn-to-autumn calendar.
   - Astronomical Canon of Ptolemy (confirming Artaxerxes I began sole rule in 465/464 B.C.).

## 2. The Relationship Between Daniel 8 & Daniel 9: "Chathak"

### The Linguistic Link
- Daniel 9:24: "Seventy weeks are determined (*nechtak*) upon thy people..."
- Hebrew root: חָתַךְ (*chathak*).
- Biblical Hapax Legomenon: Occurs only once in the Hebrew Bible.
- In post-biblical Mishnaic and Talmudic Hebrew, as well as Aramaic and Semitic cognates, *chathak* universally means "to cut off, amputate, sever, or cut out from a larger whole" (e.g., Mishnah *Chullin* 4.1; *Shabbat* 10.5; Gesenius *Hebrew-Chaldee Lexicon*, p. 314; Jastrow *Dictionary of the Targumim, Talmud, and Midrashic Literature*, p. 510).
- Gabriel had been commanded in Daniel 8:16: "Gabriel, make this man to understand the vision." At the end of Daniel 8, Daniel was sick and "none understood it" (8:27). When Gabriel reappears in Daniel 9:21–23, he says: "therefore understand the matter, and consider the vision." The 70 weeks (490 years) are severed or cut off from the earlier unexplained 2,300-day vision of Daniel 8:14.

## 3. The Christological Pinnacle: A.D. 27, A.D. 31, and A.D. 34

### The Prophetic Intervals
1. **69 Weeks (483 Years) to Messiah the Prince:**
   - 457 B.C. + 483 years:
   - 483 - 457 = 26. Adding 1 year because there is no year zero (1 B.C. transitions directly to A.D. 1) = **A.D. 27**.
   - Luke 3:1, 21–23 dates Jesus' baptism by John to the "fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar" (A.D. 27/28), when Jesus "began to be about thirty years of age." Upon His baptism, Jesus was anointed with the Holy Ghost (*Messiah* = The Anointed One), and immediately proclaimed: "The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand" (Mark 1:15).
2. **Midst of the 70th Week (3½ Years): The Cross (A.D. 31):**
   - Daniel 9:27: "in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease."
   - A.D. 27 + 3½ years = Spring of **A.D. 31** (Passover).
   - Jesus was crucified at Passover. Matthew 27:51 records that at His death, "the veil of the temple was rent in twain from the top to the bottom," signifying the divine termination of earthly animal sacrifices, fulfilled in the once-for-all sacrifice of Christ (Hebrews 9:26–28; 10:8–12).
3. **Close of the 70 Weeks (A.D. 34):**
   - A.D. 31 + 3½ years = **A.D. 34**.
   - Marked by the official rejection of the apostolic witness by the Sanhedrin, the stoning of Stephen (Acts 7), and the dispersion of the gospel to the Gentiles through the conversion of Saul of Tarsus (Acts 8:1–4; 9:1–15; 13:46).
4. **The Remainder of the 2,300 Years:**
   - 2,300 years - 490 years = 1,810 years remaining.
   - A.D. 34 + 1,810 years = **A.D. 1844**.
   - The sitting explicitly hedges this arithmetic, teaching the layers of deduction rather than claiming the date 1844 is printed in Daniel 8:14.
