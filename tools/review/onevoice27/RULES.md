# OneVoice27 — Rules for Creating Future Sheets
### Binding authoring standard (after Sheet 0)

Use this document every time you draft Sheet 1, Sheet 2, and beyond. If a draft breaks these rules, revise before teaching.

---

## 0. Mission

Sheets exist for **church teaching and evangelism** that is:
- Scripture-first
- Historicist (argued, not merely assumed)
- Centred on **Christ — redemption and restoration**
- Clear enough for serious first-time Bible students, without gutting substance into incoherence
- Free of Adventist/SDA/EGW **branding** in user-facing copy (doctrine may be shaped by that theology; labels stay biblical)

---

## 1. Governing hermeneutic (non-negotiable)

1. **Scripture interprets Scripture.** State it. Use it. Cite the principle (e.g. Isaiah 8:20; 28:10; 1 Corinthians 2:13; Luke 24:27).
2. Never teach a private code. Parallel visions, Prophets, Gospels, Epistles, and Revelation explain Daniel.
3. **Historicism is the correct framework for this course** and must be **shown from Bible study**, not left as "one option among equals."
4. Honesty is not neutrality. Label Text / History / Interpretation / Christ — then **argue** why the historicist conclusion wins.

---

## 2. The four levels (every major claim)

Every major prophetic claim must pass:

| Level | Ask | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **TEXT** | What does Scripture explicitly say? | Quote KJV accurately. Do not overclaim. |
| **HISTORY** | What can history document? | Cite real sources. Mark reconstructions. |
| **INTERPRETATION** | What does historicist Bible study conclude? | Label it. Prove it from Scripture-with-Scripture. |
| **CHRIST** | How does this serve redemption and restoration in Jesus? | Required; not optional garnish. |

Forbidden: presenting Interpretation as if it were a bare Bible quotation.

---

## 3. Required sections in every sheet (unless a sheet is pure drill)

1. **Big idea** (one sentence)
2. **TEXT** engagement with the passage(s)
3. **HISTORY** where relevant (honestly scoped)
4. **INTERPRETATION** with historicist case from Scripture interpreting Scripture
5. **CHRISTOLOGY** — redemption and/or restoration link
6. **Personal note** — apply to the reader's life without turning the sheet into therapy-speak
7. **Source checkpoints** or "Trace it yourself" tasks
8. **Student questions** with answers that match the sheet
9. **Bridge to next sheet**

Sheet 0 is the method charter. Later sheets may be shorter on method and heavier on passage — but must not abandon these elements.

---

## 4. Tone and length

1. Prefer the **long, coherent teaching sheet** over a skinny "mass cut" that becomes incoherent.
2. Warm, direct, church-ready. No sneering. No fog.
3. Be **direct** about Reformation / Counter-Reformation stakes when the topic requires it (historicism identified the papacy with the beast; Jesuit preterism/futurism relocated fulfillment to escape that identification — with proof).
4. Do not reduce history to a cartoon conspiracy; do not soft-pedal documented polemical purpose either.

---

## 5. Accuracy bars (church standard)

Before a sheet ships:

### Biblical
- [ ] All Scripture quotations verified (KJV unless a sheet explicitly uses another for comparison)
- [ ] No "the Bible says X" where X is only inference
- [ ] Scripture-interprets-Scripture links are real parallels, not forced

### Historical
- [ ] Dates and names checked against primary or standard references
- [ ] Reconstructions labelled (e.g. 605 BC, proleptic titles, accession-year explanations)
- [ ] Wycliffe-type anachronisms avoided ("before and during," not false period labels)
- [ ] Ribera dated 1590/1591; Alcázar 1614 posthumous when mentioned

### Interpretive
- [ ] Historicism argued from the passage + canon
- [ ] Day-year / 1260 / sanctuary claims show their layers (no smuggled numbers)
- [ ] Rival views named fairly, then answered — not ignored and not treated as equal winners

### Christ & life
- [ ] Redemption and restoration visible
- [ ] Personal application tethered to the text

---

## 5A. MANDATORY — Every line checked, provable, and verified

**This is a hard gate. A sheet is not shippable without it.**

### What “every line” means
1. Walk the **finished** teaching sheet after drafting — not only the hotspots.
2. Treat every teaching sentence / claim unit as a row to audit (headers and pure navigation may be N/A).
3. For each unit assign exactly one tag:
   - **TEXT** — explicit Scripture
   - **HISTORY** — documented / reconstructed (must say which)
   - **INTERPRETATION** — historicist or theological conclusion
   - **CHRIST** — canonical Christology / typology
   - **PASTORAL** — personal application
   - **N/A** — non-claim (title chrome, “read this next”)
4. For each unit assign a verdict: **ACCURATE** | **SOFTEN** | **FIX** | **N/A**
5. **FIX must be applied to the sheet before shipping.** Softens must be hedged in-text or fixed.

### What “provable and verified” means
Saying “I checked it” is **not** enough. Proof must be written down.

Required proof per unit type:
| Tag | Minimum proof |
|---|---|
| TEXT | Exact KJV (or stated version) citation; quote verified against a Bible text source (e.g. Bible Gateway / printed KJV). |
| HISTORY | Named source (chronicle, inscription, standard reference, primary author+work). Mark reconstruction if not in the biblical text. |
| INTERPRETATION | The Scripture-with-Scripture path (which passages) + why the conclusion follows; labelled as interpretation in the sheet. |
| CHRIST | NT/OT passages cited; not sold as a Daniel quotation unless Daniel says it. |
| PASTORAL | Tether named (which verse/pattern); not presented as a historical fact. |

### Required deliverable (non-optional)
Every sheet package **must** include:

`SHEETN_LINECHECK.md` containing:
1. Date of audit
2. File name + version audited (the finished sheet)
3. Method note (e.g. “sentence/claim-unit walk of finished file”)
4. Counts: ACCURATE / SOFTEN / FIX / N/A
5. Full or section-compressed unit table with **Unit | Verdict | Proof**
6. List of FIX items **and confirmation they were applied**
7. Sources used for verification (URLs or editions)

### Verification rule for agents / authors
1. Draft sheet →  
2. Line-check the finished draft →  
3. Apply FIX/SOFTEN →  
4. Re-copy ship files →  
5. Only then say the sheet is ready.

**Forbidden:**
- Claiming “every line checked” without a `SHEETN_LINECHECK.md`
- Checking only famous controversies and skipping ordinary sentences
- Auditing an outline instead of the finished prose

## 6. Counter-Reformation rule (when relevant)

When discussing prophetic schools:
1. State that historicist Protestant reading identified the **papacy** with the beast/Antichrist using Scripture with Scripture.
2. State directly that systematic Jesuit **futurism (Ribera)** and **preterism (Alcázar)** functioned as Counter-Reformation counters by relocating fulfillment away from the papal era.
3. Provide the proof trail (Reformers' identification; Ribera's future 3½-year Antichrist; Alcázar's ancient fulfillment; standard histories of interpretation).
4. Then return to why historicism still best fits Daniel — Scripture first, history second.

---

## 7. What never to do

1. Do not gut a sheet into incoherence for "simplicity."
2. Do not brand Adventist / SDA / EGW in the learner-facing text.
3. Do not use Ellen White as a proof-text in public sheets (keep Scripture as the public rule).
4. Do not teach 457 / 27 / 31 / 34 / 1844 as if printed in the verse; show the argument.
5. Do not claim "100% beyond all scholarly dispute." Claim: biblically grounded, historically careful, historicist, church-ready.
6. Do not leave historicism unproven.
7. Do not ship without a completed, evidence-backed `SHEETN_LINECHECK.md`.
8. Do not tell the operator “every line was checked” unless that linecheck file exists and matches the shipped sheet.

---

## 8. Delivery package for each new sheet

Ship **four** files (linecheck is mandatory):
1. `SHEETN_....md` — the teaching sheet
2. `SHEETN_PROOF.md` — proof of contested claims / changes / hedges
3. `SHEETN_LINECHECK.md` — **every-line audit with verdicts and proof** (see §5A)
4. Update this rules doc only if a new standing rule is discovered

A sheet without item 3 is **not approved for church use**.
