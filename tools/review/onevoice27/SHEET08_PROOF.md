## 1. The Scope of Daniel 8 & the Inadequacy of Antiochus IV Epiphanes

### The Contested Preterist Claim
Preterist commentators (following Porphyry and Alcázar) claim that the little horn of Daniel 8 is exclusively Syrian Seleucid king Antiochus IV Epiphanes, and that the 2,300 "evenings-mornings" are 1,150 literal days (or 2,300 literal half-days) fulfilled during Antiochus's desecration of the second temple between 167 and 164 B.C.

### Biblical Refutation & Grammatical Proof
1. **The Magnitude of Growth:**
   - The Medo-Persian Ram became "great" (*higdil*, Dan 8:4).
   - The Greek Goat became "very great" (*higdil me’od*, Dan 8:8).
   - The Little Horn became "exceeding great" (*gadol yeter*, Dan 8:9).
   - Antiochus Epiphanes did not surpass the Persian Empire or Alexander's Empire in military power, territory, or historical impact. Rome (in its pagan and papal phases) began small and grew far greater than both Greece and Persia, conquering Egypt (south), Syria (east), and Judea (the pleasant land).
2. **The Terminal Horizon of the Vision:**
   - Daniel 8:17: "Understand, O son of man: for at the time of the end (*le’eth-qets*) shall be the vision."
   - Daniel 8:19: "for at the time appointed the end shall be (*lemo’ed qets*)."
   - Antiochus Epiphanes died in 164 B.C. in Persia—centuries before the first advent of Christ and millenia before the "time of the end."
3. **The 2,300 Evenings-Mornings (*'Ereb Boqer*):**
   - Genesis 1:5, 8, 13, 19, 23, 31: "the evening and the morning were the first day" (*'ereb boqer yom ehad*). An evening-morning is a single compound unit designating a full biblical day.
   - Daniel 8:26: Gabriel calls the 2,300 span "the vision of the evening and the morning" (*mar'eh ha'ereb wehabboqer*), treating it as an indivisible prophetic period.
   - 2,300 literal days (approx. 6 years and 4 months) does not match any documented historical interval under Antiochus. The temple was desecrated for precisely 3 literal years (1 Maccabees 1:54, 59; 4:52–54: 25th of Kislev 167 B.C. to 25th of Kislev 164 B.C. = 1,080 or 1,095 days).
4. **Sanctuary Justification / Cleansing (*Nitsdaq*):**
   - Daniel 8:14: "then shall the sanctuary be cleansed" (Hebrew: *wenitsdaq qodesh* — Niphal stem of *tsadaq*, "shall be vindicated, justified, restored to its rightful state").
   - Ties directly to Leviticus 16:16, 30, the annual Day of Atonement (*Yom Kippur*), when the sanctuary is cleansed from all uncleanness and sins of Israel.
   - Reference: Gerhard F. Hasel, *The 'Little Horn,' the Heavenly Sanctuary, and the Time of the End: A Study of Daniel 8:9–14* (DARCOM, 1986).
