## 3. Sitting-Specific Probes (Plan §1.4)

### Probe 8.1: Gabriel's Explicit Animal Identifications
- **Lesson Presentation:** Gabriel explicitly names the animals in Daniel 8:20–22:
  - *Ram with two horns:* Media and Persia (higher horn coming up last = Persian predominance under Cyrus).
  - *Rough goat:* Greece; large horn = first king (Alexander the Great, 331 B.C. victory at Gaugamela).
  - *Four horns replacing the broken horn:* Four Diadochi successor kingdoms (Cassander, Lysimachus, Seleucus, Ptolemy).
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** Flawless. Scripture interprets its own symbols; no private guesswork permitted. Furthermore, the lesson notes that the ram and goat are the two animals of Leviticus 16 (Day of Atonement), connecting the vision's imagery to its ultimate sanctuary climax.

### Probe 8.2: Threefold Refutation of Antiochus IV Epiphanes
- **Lesson Presentation:** The lesson systematically proves why Antiochus Epiphanes fails as the primary fulfiller of Daniel 8:
  1. *Progression of Greatness:* The Medo-Persian ram was "great" (*gadol*, 8:4); the Grecian goat was "very great" (*higdil*, 8:8); but the little horn became "exceedingly great" (*yigdal-me'od*, 8:9), challenging the Prince of the host (Christ). Antiochus was a minor Seleucid monarch who paid tribute to Rome and retreated from Egypt under Roman orders; he was never greater than Alexander or Cyrus.
  2. *Time Horizon:* Gabriel twice declares that the vision refers to the "time of the end" (8:17, 19; see also 8:26). An event in the 160s B.C. cannot exhaust a vision explicitly locked to the time of the end.
  3. *Chronological Span & Sanctuary Cleansing:* The 2,300 evening-mornings (~6.3 literal years) do not match Antiochus's ~3-year temple desecration (167–164 B.C.). More decisively, the Hebrew verb is *nitsdaq* (justified, vindicated, restored to rightful state), pointing to the celestial Day of Atonement rather than an earthly altar repair.
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** Endorsed by SDABC Vol. 4, pp. 841–846 and Dr. Gerhard Hasel (*AUSS* 13, 1975). This is the definitive BRI apologetic refuting preterism.

### Probe 8.3: Two-Phase Rome (Pagan and Papal)
- **Lesson Presentation:** The lesson teaches that the little horn of Daniel 8 represents Rome in two successive phases:
  - *Pagan Phase:* Grew horizontally toward south, east, and the Glorious Land; crucified the Prince of the host; destroyed Jerusalem and the earthly temple in A.D. 70.
  - *Papal Phase:* Grew vertically toward heaven; removed the *tamid* by substituting an earthly priesthood, confessional booths, and the sacrifice of the Mass for Christ's continual heavenly mediation (Heb 7:25; 8:1–2); cast truth to the ground.
  - Cites *SDA Bible Commentary* 4:841: *"This little horn represents Rome in both its phases, pagan and papal"* and ABSG Daniel 2020 Q1, week 9.
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** Verified. This reflects the standard SDA historicist consensus.

### Probe 8.4: Linguistic Precision: *Tamid* and *Nitsdaq*
- **Lesson Presentation:**
  - *Tamid (H8548):* Denotes the continual, daily priestly mediation. In this vision, it represents Christ's standing high-priestly intercession in heaven, which the horn attempts to obscure and usurp.
  - *Nitsdaq (H6663):* Translated "cleansed" in the KJV, but literally meaning "to be justified, vindicated, made right." Contrasted with *taher* (ceremonial/physical washing). Aligns with the investigative judgment of Dan 7:9–14 and the Day of Atonement of Leviticus 16.
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** Rigorous and exact. Aligns with Hasel's linguistic studies on Daniel 8:14 and BRI publications.

### Probe 8.5: The 1844 American Experience & Sola Scriptura Distinction
- **Lesson Presentation:** The callout box `"First principles • Why 1844 is an American year on this map"` and the map nodes present the history of the Millerite movement with balanced candor:
  - William Miller's lectures from 1831; Himes printing *Signs of the Times* (1840); Samuel S. Snow preaching 22 October 1844 at Exeter from Leviticus 16 and the Karaite calendar.
  - The Great Disappointment is explicitly named: *"a mistaken event (they had made the earth the sanctuary), not a vanished number."*
  - The aftermath: Hiram Edson and early believers realized the sanctuary of Dan 8:14 is in heaven (Heb 8:1–2; 9:23–24).
  - Explicit sources cited: Miller's *Apology and Defence*, Himes's *Signs of the Times*, Snow's *True Midnight Cry*, and Froom's *Prophetic Faith of Our Fathers*, Vol. 4.
  - Glossary instruction: *"Check it as their account, then test it in Hebrews 8–9."*
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** Exemplary. Distinguishes historical human experience from biblical canon, maintaining Sola Scriptura while honoring pioneer history.

### Probe 8.6: Calvary vs. 1844 in Christology
- **Lesson Presentation:** The Christology pane explicitly clarifies:
  *"The Day of Atonement cleansing of the sanctuary in 1844 is Christ’s final priestly work, not a repetition of the cross. On Calvary, Jesus offered the once-for-all, all-sufficient sacrifice for human sin, paying our penalty in full (Heb 9:26). But an atonement requires both sacrifice and priestly ministry."*
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** Crucial theological safeguard. Fully upholds Fundamental Belief 24 and prevents any accusation of minimizing the finished, all-sufficient atonement of the cross.
