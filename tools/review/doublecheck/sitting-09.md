## 3. Sitting-Specific Probes (Plan §1.4)

### Probe 9.1: Meaning and Exegetical Force of *Chathak* (Dan 9:24)
- **Lesson Presentation:** The callout box `"First principles • What chathak means: Severed from the parent line"` explains:
  - When Gabriel touches Daniel (9:21–23), he commands him to *"consider the vision"*—referring back to the only unexplained vision of the previous chapter (the 2,300 days of Dan 8:14, 27).
  - Gabriel announces: *"Seventy weeks are determined [chathak] for your people"* (9:24).
  - *Chathak* is a biblical *hapax legomenon* (occurring only once in the Hebrew Bible). In Talmudic, rabbinic, and cognate Semitic languages, its root meaning is *"to cut off, amputate, or sever"* a portion from a larger whole.
  - Therefore, seventy weeks (490 years) are cut off from the parent 2,300-day timeline, proving both prophecies share the identical starting point in Persian history.
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** Endorsed by SDABC Vol. 4, pp. 851–852 and DARCOM Vol. 3. This is the foundational historicist demonstration establishing the link between Daniel 8 and Daniel 9.

### Probe 9.2: Identification of the Starting Anchor: Four Decrees Compared
- **Lesson Presentation:** The callout box `"First principles • Four decrees, one 'restore': Identifying the starting anchor"` provides a comparative table of the four Persian return decrees:
  1. *Cyrus (538/537 B.C., Ezra 1:1–4):* Rebuilding of the temple only; no municipal restoration.
  2. *Darius I (520 B.C., Ezra 6:1–12):* Reaffirmed Cyrus's temple decree; no civil authority.
  3. *Artaxerxes I (457 B.C., Ezra 7:11–26):* Authorized Ezra to appoint magistrates and judges, institute civil law, enforce judicial penalties, and establish autonomous Jewish government. This is the only decree that both *restores* civil order and *builds* the city.
  4. *Artaxerxes I to Nehemiah (444 B.C., Neh 2:1–8):* Personal permit to repair physical walls; not a new decree establishing civil status.
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** Flawless. Supported by SDABC Vol. 3 on Ezra 7 and Siegfried H. Horn & Lynn H. Wood, *The Chronology of Ezra 7*.

### Probe 9.3: Verification of the No-Year-Zero Arithmetic Chain
- **Lesson Presentation:** The lesson walks the arithmetic step-by-step:
  - 69 weeks = 483 prophetic days = 483 solar years.
  - Autumn 457 B.C. + 483 years: Start at autumn 457 B.C. Add 457 years $\rightarrow$ Autumn 1 B.C. Since 1 B.C. to A.D. 1 is 1 year (no year zero), adding the remaining 26 years brings us to **Autumn A.D. 27** ($483 - 457 + 1 = 27$).
  - Autumn A.D. 27: Jesus is baptized and anointed by the Holy Spirit (Luke 3:1, 21–23; Acts 10:38), proclaiming: *"The time is fulfilled"* (Mark 1:15).
  - Midst of the week: 3.5 years after Autumn A.D. 27 $\rightarrow$ **Spring A.D. 31** (Passover). Messiah is cut off; the temple veil is torn from top to bottom (Matt 27:51).
  - Close of 70 weeks: Spring A.D. 31 + 3.5 years $\rightarrow$ **Autumn A.D. 34** (Stephen's martyrdom; gospel turns to Gentiles).
  - Remaining balance: $2,300 - 490 = 1,810$ years. Autumn A.D. 34 + 1,810 years $\rightarrow$ **Autumn 1844 A.D.** (October 22, 1844 by Karaite Yom Kippur reckoning).
- **SDA Historicist Evaluation:** 100% mathematically and historically sound. Aligns with DARCOM Vol. 3 and *The Great Controversy*, p. 326–329.

### Probe 9.4: Grammatical Refutation of the Dispensational Futurist Gap
