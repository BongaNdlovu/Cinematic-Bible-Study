### 4. Arithmetic verification
- **Daniel 9:24–25 seventy weeks**: $70 \times 7 = 490$ days.
  - As literal wall-clock days: $\frac{490}{30} \approx 16.33$ months. Text states: "about sixteen months — not enough time to rebuild a city and reach the cross." Accurate.
  - As prophetic years: 490 years.
- **Daniel 7:25 1,260 days**: 1,260 prophetic years.
  - Stated historical span: A.D. 538 to A.D. 1798.
  - Calculation: $538 + 1260 = 1798$. Accurate.
- **Daniel 8:14 2,300 days**: 2,300 prophetic years.
  - Stated historical span: 457 B.C. to A.D. 1844.
  - Calculation (no year zero): $-457 + 2300 = 1843$; crossing B.C./A.D. boundary without year 0 adds 1: $1843 + 1 = 1844$. Accurate.
