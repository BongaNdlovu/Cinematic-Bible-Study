OPERATOR TELEMETRY • EXEGESIS ANALYTICS

Study insights

Moderator only. How many people open and finish each sitting, how far they read, how they answer, and what they tell us. Nothing here is sold or used for advertising.

Sign in with the moderator account from the Account page to open this page.

How these numbers are counted
  • A person is a signed-in account, or a browser for guests. Someone who reads on two devices without signing in counts twice.
  • Opened and finished count each person once per sitting, however many times they come back. Hover a funnel row for the raw totals.
  • Leave out my own activity drops events from your account and from every browser you have signed in on. Other moderators still count.
  • Quiz answers are only recorded for signed-in readers, because guests cannot check an answer.
  • Graduates are readers whose saved progress holds all eleven sittings. That progress is saved by the reader's browser, and moderator accounts can finish sittings without answering, so check a name before you rely on it.
  • Anyone can send study events to the site, so treat a sudden spike with care before acting on it.
